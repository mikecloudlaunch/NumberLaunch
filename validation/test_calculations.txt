VALIDATION OF NUMBERLAUNCH TAX CALCULATOR AGAINST ATO CALCULATOR

Testing 2024-2025 rates for various income scenarios:

SCENARIO 1: $60,000 annual income, no deductions, no HECS debt
------------------------------------------------------------

https://www.ato.gov.au/calculators-and-tools/simple-tax-calculator/
