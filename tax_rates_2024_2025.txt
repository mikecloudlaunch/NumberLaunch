Current Australian tax rates 2024-2025:

Resident tax rates 2024–25:
$0 – $18,200: Nil
$18,201 – $45,000: 19 cents for each $1 over $18,200
$45,001 – $135,000: $5,092 plus 30 cents for each $1 over $45,000
$135,001 – $190,000: $32,092 plus 37 cents for each $1 over $135,000
$190,001 and over: $52,467 plus 45 cents for each $1 over $190,000

Key changes from 2022-2023:
1. The 32.5% bracket has been lowered to 30%
2. The $120,000 threshold has increased to $135,000
3. The $180,000 threshold has increased to $190,000
